What are cookies?

Cookies are small files that websites save on your device. Websites can also save small amounts of information in your browser's storage, which works in a similar way. This policy covers both, and we call them all "cookies" below.

We keep this to a minimum. We don't use any advertising or analytics cookies, such as Google Analytics.

Cookies we always use

These are needed for the website to work, so they don't need your consent.

Name Set by What it's for How long it lasts
tmph-cookie-consent Us Remembers whether you accepted or rejected optional cookies. Until you change your choice or clear your browser's data
tmph-newsletter Us Remembers that you've signed up to or closed our mailing list popup, so we don't keep showing it. Until you clear your browser's data
_bookwhen_admin_session Bookwhen Only on our event booking pages. Makes the booking form and basket work. Until you close your browser

Optional cookies

We only use these if you click "Accept" on our cookie banner. The website works in exactly the same way if you reject them.

Name Set by What it's for How long it lasts
ml_guid MailerLite, our mailing list provider A random identifier that lets MailerLite recognise returning visitors, to help us understand how people use our website. Until you reject optional cookies or clear your browser's data

Other services on our website

  • Google Maps. Our map is provided by Google. Google may set its own cookies, for example if you're signed in to a Google account or interact with the map.
  • Google Fonts. Our text font is loaded from Google. This doesn't set cookies, but Google receives your device's IP address when the font loads.
  • Mailing list sign-up. When you join our mailing list, your email address is sent to MailerLite. Signing up doesn't set any cookies, and you can unsubscribe at any time.

You can read more in each provider's own privacy policy:

Managing your cookies

You can change your choice at any time using the "Change cookie settings" button above, or "Cookie settings" at the bottom of every page.

You can also delete or block cookies in your browser's settings. The Information Commissioner's Office has more about how cookies work and how to control them.

We'll update this page if we change the cookies we use. The date at the top shows when it was last updated.
