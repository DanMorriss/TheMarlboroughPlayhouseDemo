Plan your visit

Where to find us

The Old Chapel, The Parade, SN8 1NE (we're between the cinema and the fire station)

Opening hours

Monday to Saturday, 9am - 4:30pm. No set session times, open play all day.

Parking

2 hours free parking right outside, or longer paid parking less than a minute's walk away at Kennet Place or Waitrose car park.

No booking needed

Mornings are often busy, so feel free to text or WhatsApp to reserve a table. Just let us know the date, time and how many adults and children are coming. Let us know if it's a special occasion too!

What's here?

Play

A dedicated baby area for non-crawlers, role play rooms, a cosy den and plenty to explore once they're on the move. Every corner is reachable for even the littlest explorers.

Café

Hot and cold drinks, cakes (with gluten free, dairy free and vegan options), pastries and snack plates for little ones, plus proper coffee for the grown-ups.

Ceramics

Our ceramics shop is perfect for little ones, older kids and adults alike. Paint a mug, vase or trinket at your table, or take one home to paint there. Either way, leave it with us for firing and it'll be ready to take home and enjoy.
